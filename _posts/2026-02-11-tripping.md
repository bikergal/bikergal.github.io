I often consume myself with the need to control.
lists, calendars, itineraries, agendas. 
when do I live in the present? 


I notice my body posture tenses when I'm trying extra hard to control. 
at Tilton House, I squeeze my abs and legs and curl up into a ball on the couch. 
I try very hard not to take up space. then I catch myself doing this and relax. 
I feel better instantly. 
even if I was taking up too much space, someone would speak up. 
no one wants their guests uncomfortable. 
I don't want me uncomfortable. 


I'm practicing extending the same care I give to others unto myself.
