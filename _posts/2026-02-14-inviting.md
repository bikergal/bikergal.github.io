anxiety tells me I'm not welcome. 
it makes me crave extra reassurance that I belong in a space. 
I put on my brave thumbs and invite myself along. 
I breathe a sigh of relief when I get a response. 
was that so hard? 

I play the other scenario out in my head. 
what if they preferred not to have me join? 
they would say no or not this time. 
then I would say oh well and move on or try to plan for something else. 

the other option was saying nothing. 
this I think is worst of all. 
I would regret it for days, possibly years.
