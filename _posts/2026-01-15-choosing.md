## brain dump
choice is a strange thing. our brains are making thousands of them every day.
some parenting advice insists that giving children choices offers them the opportunity to exercise freedom.
I think dental hygienists employ this when asking about toothpaste and fluoride flavors or toothbrush colors. 
we're just little control freaks; it makes us feel better.

I judge myself for many of my decisions, and a lot of it is pretty unfair. 
I apparently also really love to pretend to mindread. spoiler alert, I'm not good at it.
I'm practicing questioning if I actually *know* what someone think about a decision I've made, or if I'm just projecting my worst fear.
then I remind myself that I'm lucky because most people are generally understanding. 

I truly am lucky, and I am noticing that it's been hitting extra hard lately. 
I just can't stop noticing how much abundance there is in my life. 
I'm doing my best to hold on and not overthink it.
