scarcity mindset is catching up to me again. 
I zoom around the house, feeling the pressure of all the things I'm putting on my plate. 
I sit down for few minutes and remember it doesn't have to be like this. 
I still feel uncomfortable but call the recognition to pause a win. 

I notice I feel the pull towards online browsing when I feel overwhelmed. 
it feels like an easy way to convince myself still being productive by gathering information (whether useful or not) while outputting a low level of energy. 
the reality is that using a higher level of activation energy for a short burst of time (i.e. hauling my ass to the gym) will result in me feeling much better. 

practicing being present in the now requires a bit of a delicate balance from me. 
while anxiety could get me out of bed at the worry of wasting away, I can convince myself that presence says I'm comfortable right now and should stay home.
