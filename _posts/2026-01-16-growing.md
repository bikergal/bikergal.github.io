## brain dump
the pro and con of being more present is not thinking about the future.
for an overthinker, it's truly a welcome break.

every now & then I remember something that the Overthinker Brain wanted to worry about.
obsessively checking my bank & retirement accounts, worrying about kids I don't have, documenting very purchase.
I remember that it felt urgent to occupy so much space and attention with these things.

I'll still probably always worry about money and family, but it's feeling more manageable. 
I'm a little afraid of going too much in the other direction.
lately I realized that the Baby Fever has largely faded although I still like the idea of kids being part of my life.
I just haven't decided in what capacity yet.
