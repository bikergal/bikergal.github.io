## brain dump
last night a friend celebrated her 30th. 
I'd not seen her since her last birthday, and I was feeling quite anxious about going at all. 
it turns out I had nothing to worry about, but I think that's largely because of the mindset prep I did beforehand. 

I realized at previous parties, I was always worried about being interesting & fun. 
I fixated so much on that, and I ended up having awkward convos with new people or just not talking at all when I wish I had. 
this time, I just focused on being supportive & learning about her friends. 
it felt so much more natural to just prioritize being present for someone instead of tuning them out while writing my next joke. 

I want to remind myself of that next time I'm scared.
