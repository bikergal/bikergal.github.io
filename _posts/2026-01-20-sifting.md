## brain dump
I truly have so much abundance. 

I'm lucky to be able to have so much choice available to me.

I think bout how my parents reinforced the narrative that we didn't have resources. 
In their eyes, we never had enough time, money, things, space. 
While it was true that our resources were often limited, I wonder how alternate fing would shape a different priority. 
after all, we had more money than most people on the planet, but these of coures had to be allocated to certain things. 
when I would express a want that couldn't be fulfilled, I wonder what it would like to have a parent brainstorm alternatives with me. 

I wonder if the lack of this ultimately fostered my creativity; if I was determined enough to want something done or created, I would find a way to do it myself. 
one example was with interior design. I was always interested in creating a space that felt more me. and me was always changing. 
my mom would yell at me whenever I rearranged the furniture in the bedroom. "ayy that's heavy! you're not going to be able to have kids!"
