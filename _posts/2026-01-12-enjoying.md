## brain dump
I gotta say, things have been pretty great lately.
those vitamin d gummies are doing some heavy lifting.

I was really proud of myself for doing a lot of meal prep yesterday & today. even more than that, I was really proud that I actually let myself be lazybones and genuinely let myself relax.
normally I would scold myself and say out loud "I'm lazybones" while sitting on the couch all tense & worrying about the more "productive" things I could be doing.
today I ended work at a time when I felt it was appropriate, made cookies, made dinner (yes in that order). I took care of a few chores, and then I decided I wanted to sit in the new nook I'd set up recently.
then I did something I haven't done for a while; I spent a few of hours just listening to classical music while sewing.

I started doing this in 4th grade shortly after I started menstruating. it was summer, and as usual, we were stuck at home. I'd tune the radio into whatever was interesting.
on a hot & humid afternoon in Georgia, I could imagine I was in an air-conditioned auditorium craning my neck up at a concertmaster.
today, Four Seasons made an appearance while I darned a sock. I felt a real desire to hear it performed in person soon (Baltimore this weekend anyone?).
I feel lucky lately. and just so damn grateful for where I am and the abundance I'm in.
